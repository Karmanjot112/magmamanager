# Community Panels v2.1.0

## Invite check panel
Open `/setup` → **Invites**, choose a channel, then press **Post Invite Button**. Members can press **Check My Invites** to see their own invite stats privately.

## Review panel
Open `/setup` → **Reviews**, choose a channel, then press **Post Review Panel**. Members press **⭐ Write a Review**, choose a rating from 1 to 5, and enter a description. The bot posts/updates that member's review and refreshes the overall average automatically.

All public component views are persistent across bot restarts.
