# Welcomer & Auto Role — v1.2.7

Open `/setup` and choose **Welcomer**. Configure a text channel, message and optional auto role.

Supported placeholders: `{mention}`, `{username}`, `{display_name}`, `{user_id}`, `{server}`, `{member_count}`, `{account_age}`, `{date}`, `{time}`.

The bot requires **Manage Roles** and its highest role must be above the selected automatic role. Administrator and managed roles are blocked.
